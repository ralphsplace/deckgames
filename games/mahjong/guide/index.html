How to play Mahjong Solitaire

A one-player puzzle with mahjong tiles: take matching pairs off the pile until the table is clear. Every deal can be cleared.

Free tiles

144 tiles are stacked in the shape of a turtle. You can only take a tile that is free: nothing on top of it, and nothing next to it on at least one side (left or right). Tiles that aren't free are shaded.

Matching

Click a free tile, then a free tile that's the same. Both come off the table. Click a tile again to put it back.

Flowers and seasons

Everything matches only its exact twin, with two exceptions: any flower matches any flower, and any season matches any season.

When you're stuck

Not sure what's left? Hint lights up a pair you can take.

Take pairs carelessly and you can run out of moves. Then press Shuffle: the tiles still on the table are dealt again into the same places, in an order that can always be cleared. Or use Undo to go back and try different pairs.

Buttons

  • Undo puts the last pair back, as far back as you like.
  • Restart deal starts this same deal over; New game deals a new one.
  • The line underneath shows tiles left, moves, shuffles, the deal number and the time. Your best time is kept for games cleared without shuffling.
